Troubleshooting guide

Why is my aircon leaking water?

A small drip can point to a simple blockage—or a problem that will keep returning until the underlying cause is repaired.

Vision Air Singapore · About 6 minutes

First: switch the unit off

If water is dripping near a power point, wiring or electronic device, turn the aircon off and keep the area dry. Place a container or towel below the leak, but do not open the electrical compartment.

Safety firstWater and electricity are a dangerous combination. If water is entering an electrical fitting, isolate the affected circuit only if you can do so safely and arrange professional help.

Six common reasons an aircon leaks

1. A blocked drainage pipe

Dust, algae and residue can build up inside the drain line. Condensation then backs up into the fan coil unit and eventually spills into the room. This is one of the most common causes of recurring leaks.

2. A dirty filter or cooling coil

Restricted airflow can make the coil excessively cold. Moisture may freeze on the coil and later melt faster than the drain system can remove it.

3. A frozen evaporator coil

Besides dirt, low airflow, fan problems and refrigerant issues can cause icing. If you see ice, switching the unit off allows it to thaw—but the underlying cause still needs diagnosis.

4. A damaged or displaced drain pan

The drain pan catches condensation and directs it into the pipe. Cracks, corrosion or poor alignment can send water over the edge instead.

5. Poor pipe gradient or installation

A drain pipe needs a suitable fall so water can move away by gravity. Sagging pipes, incorrect routing or an indoor unit that is not level can allow water to collect.

6. Condensation around poorly insulated piping

Cold refrigerant pipes can collect moisture when insulation is damaged, too thin or poorly sealed. The water may appear to come from the aircon even though it is forming along the pipe route.

Checks you can make safely

Turn the unit off and dry the surrounding area
Check whether the filter is visibly dusty
Look for ice without removing electrical covers
Note whether the leak starts immediately or after hours

Take a short video showing where the water appears and send it with the unit model, number of affected units and your postal code. This helps the service team prepare for the visit.

When servicing is not enough

A routine service may resolve a straightforward drainage blockage. However, repeated leakage can indicate damaged drainage components, incorrect pipe routing, heavy internal build-up or another fault. Ask the technician to explain the cause—not only clear the immediate water.

Frequently asked questions

Can I continue running a leaking aircon?

It is safer to switch it off. Continued use can damage walls, furniture and the aircon itself, especially if the leak is near electrical components.

Does a leak mean the aircon needs gas?

Not necessarily. Drainage and airflow problems are common causes. Refrigerant should only be added after a technician has identified why the system needs it.

Will cleaning the filter stop the leak?

It may improve airflow, but it will not repair a blocked drain, damaged pan, pipe-gradient problem or refrigerant fault.
