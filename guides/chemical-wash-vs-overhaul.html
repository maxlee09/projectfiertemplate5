Servicing guide

Chemical wash or chemical overhaul?

The names are often used loosely. What matters is which components are removed, how they are cleaned and whether the service addresses the actual problem.

Vision Air Singapore · About 7 minutes

Start with the least invasive suitable service

A well-maintained unit does not need a chemical overhaul every time. General servicing is usually the starting point for routine maintenance. Deeper cleaning should be recommended because of the unit’s condition—not simply because it costs more.

Ask for the scope in writingDifferent contractors may use “chemical wash,” “chemical cleaning” and “overhaul” differently. Confirm which parts will be dismantled, what cleaner will be used and whether the drainage system is included.

How the options compare

ServiceWhat normally happensBest suited for
General servicingFilters, coil surface, blower area and drain connection are cleaned and operating components are checked.Regular preventive maintenance and units without heavy internal build-up.
Chemical washA cleaning solution is applied to accessible components to loosen stubborn dirt. The exact dismantling level varies by contractor.Units that remain dirty or inefficient after routine cleaning but do not require full disassembly.
Chemical overhaulThe indoor unit is taken down and major parts such as the cooling coil, blower wheel, drain pan, covers and filters are dismantled for thorough cleaning.Severe dirt or algae, persistent odour, restricted airflow, repeated leakage or cooling problems linked to heavy build-up.

Signs that deeper cleaning may be justified

Weak airflow after normal servicing
Persistent musty or sour odour
Visible heavy dirt on the blower or coil
Recurring drainage blockage or leakage

These symptoms can also have other causes. For example, poor cooling may come from an electrical, fan, sensor or refrigerant problem. Cleaning cannot repair a faulty component.

Questions to ask before approving an overhaul

  1. What did you observe that makes an overhaul necessary?
  2. Which components will be removed and cleaned?
  3. Is the outdoor unit included?
  4. Does the quoted price include labour, transport and GST?
  5. What guarantee applies after the service?
  6. What happens if cleaning does not resolve the problem?

Frequently asked questions

How often should I get a chemical overhaul?

There is no universal schedule. The need depends on usage, environment, maintenance history and the actual condition of the unit. Routine servicing should not automatically become an overhaul.

Will an overhaul make every old aircon cold again?

No. It can restore performance lost to severe dirt, but it cannot fix a weak compressor, faulty electronics, damaged fan motor or refrigerant leak.

Is chemical cleaning safe?

It should be performed by trained personnel using a suitable cleaner, proper rinsing and protection for nearby surfaces. Ask how the chemical residue will be removed.
