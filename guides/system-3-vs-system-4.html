Buying guide

System 3 or System 4: which fits your home?

The right answer depends on more than the number of rooms. Consider where you spend time, which units run together and whether future flexibility matters.

Vision Air Singapore · About 7 minutes

What the system number means

A System 3 connects three indoor fan coil units to one outdoor condenser. A System 4 connects four. The number does not tell you whether the condenser has enough capacity for your intended room combination—that requires proper sizing.

Simultaneous use mattersIf several rooms run at the same time, the outdoor unit must distribute its capacity among them. Tell the surveyor which rooms are normally used together, especially at night.

Choose based on how the home is used

System 3 may suit you when:

  • You mainly need cooling in three bedrooms.
  • The living room uses a separate system or does not require air-conditioning.
  • You want to control the initial installation and ongoing servicing cost.
  • Your household rarely needs more than three spaces cooled.

System 4 may suit you when:

  • You want three bedrooms and the living area connected to one condenser.
  • A home office or study is used regularly.
  • Outdoor condenser space is limited and another separate system is impractical.
  • You expect the fourth room to be used enough to justify the extra unit.

Five factors the site survey should check

Room size, ceiling height and afternoon sun
How many rooms run simultaneously
Outdoor condenser location and ventilation
Pipe route, drainage and trunking appearance
Electrical supply and isolator requirements
Access for future servicing and repair

Do not size by floor area alone

Two rooms of the same size may have different cooling needs because of sun exposure, windows, occupants, electronics and how often doors are opened. Oversizing can also be undesirable: a unit may cool rapidly but cycle frequently and control humidity less effectively.

Installation quality affects the long-term result

Ask what materials are included in the quoted price. Vision Air publishes the use of 22 SWG copper pipes, half-inch thermal insulation for standard installations and 16 mm drainage pipes. A free site survey can also identify whether brackets, electrical work or non-standard routing are required.

Plan for the actual homeA clear quotation should show the indoor-unit combination, outdoor model, pipe route, materials, additional charges and warranties—not only a bundle price.

Frequently asked questions

Does System 4 always use more electricity?

Not automatically. Consumption depends on which units are running, temperature settings, equipment efficiency, room conditions and maintenance. An unused indoor unit does not cool the room simply because it is connected.

Can all four rooms run at once?

They can, but the condenser’s capacity and the selected indoor-unit combination determine performance when demand is high. Discuss simultaneous use during the survey.

Can I install System 3 now and add another unit later?

Normally, a System 3 condenser is designed for its supported configuration. Adding a fourth unit later may require replacing or adding equipment, so consider future needs before installation.
